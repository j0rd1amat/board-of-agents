A Sunday newspaper about my friends, written by agents

During the week, agents read my WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram and Gmail so I don't have to scroll. Every Sunday at 9:00 another agent turns what they found into three pages: who is moving, who is hiring, who has news, and who I should call this week.

  • Read-only.The agents never post, like or reply.
  • No quote, no fact.Every line traces back to the exact words someone wrote.
  • It stays on my laptop.Health, politics and money are left out.

The kit has the prompt, the rules and the script that prints the paper, plus a setup prompt that builds your own with you.
