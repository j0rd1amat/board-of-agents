# Outreach rules

Every run reads these first. A note from another agent can narrow them, never widen them.

## Who may be written to

- Only people on the list, and only companies scored [6] or more.
- Up to [3] people per company, one at a time. The second person is written to only if the first stays silent after the sequence ends.
- Never anyone who replied, bounced, opted out or said no in any thread. The sequence stops at a person's first reply.
- Never a company on the never-contact list (`never.txt`: your employer, clients you may not approach).

## Warm before cold

- Where I know someone at a company, that person hears from me first, in a personal message.
- While that message is open, nobody else at the company gets a cold email. Hold for [10] days, then decide.
- Before writing to a connection, check their profile. If they have changed company, skip them.

## Two domains

- Cold email goes out only from the second domain, [get-yourcompany.com]. Never from the main one.
- The second domain is warmed up before its first cold email, about two weeks.
- Never connect the main domain's mailboxes to a warm-up tool.

## Caps

- Cold email: [10] a day in week 1, [20] a day in week 2, then [30 to 40], never more than [50]. Follow-ups count toward the cap.
- One recipient per email, never a group. Plain text, no links and no attachments in the first email.
- Only the approved text, with its blanks filled in and checked.

## Stop rules

Stop all cold sending and notify me if any of these happens. Resume only when I say so.

- Bounces pass [3%] of sends.
- Anyone reports spam or complains.
- The warm-up tool's spam rate goes above [3%] or its inbox placement falls below [90%].

## What stays a draft

Write it, do not send it, and tell me, when a message would:

- commit to a price, discount, contract or legal term that is not in the price list;
- answer a complaint, a legal question or a journalist.

## Replies

- A reply is answered in the same thread.
- No reply after [5] working days: one short nudge. After that one nudge, stop.
- A request for a meeting: send the booking link, and tell me.
