# The prompt, without the script

Paste this into Claude together with two files: your `Connections.csv` from LinkedIn and your list of companies.

---

Here is my LinkedIn Connections.csv and a list of companies I care about. Find every connection who works at one of them. Match the company name as it is written in the file, and put doubtful matches in a separate list instead of guessing. Group people into three: decision-maker, senior, everyone else. Use only what is in the two files. Draft nothing and send nothing until I pick the names.

---

"Companies I care about" can be customers you want, employers you would work for, or investors.

Once you have picked names, a second prompt for the messages:

---

For each person I picked, draft one short message from me. Use only what the file says about them and what I tell you about how we know each other; if you know nothing about how we met, ask me instead of inventing it. Say in one sentence why I am writing and ask one easy question. No pitch, no link. Show me every draft and send nothing.
